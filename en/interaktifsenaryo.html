How it works

What kind of stories?

Multiple-choice stories.

Each series has a few short episodes and each episode ends with a choice. Sometimes you are in the middle of a real event; sometimes you are a character building a life or a career from scratch.

Our principles

With respect for the truth.

  • Events are based on public official reports and reliable sources; we never invent what is unknown.
  • We respect those who lost their lives: we tell these stories to understand and learn, not for sensation.
  • The choice moments are a fictional game; at the end of real-event series we clearly state what really happened.
  • For fictional stories, we say so clearly on screen.
