Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · This is a translation; the Turkish version prevails.

This policy explains how GGame (ggame.com.tr) handles data on this website and in GGame Studio, the internal app GGame uses to manage its own social media accounts (Gelin Bi Bakalım, GGame and İnteraktif Senaryo).

1. Website

This site is informational; it has no accounts, forms or cookie-based tracking. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, during which your browser's IP address may be sent to Google.

2. What does GGame Studio do?

GGame Studio is a non-public tool used only to manage GGame's own accounts (the YouTube channels, Instagram accounts and Facebook pages of the Gelin Bi Bakalım, GGame and İnteraktif Senaryo brands):

  • It uploads videos we produce ourselves to these accounts and schedules their publication.
  • It reads statistics of our own content (views, likes, saves, shares).
  • It reads public comments on our own posts (e.g. to count viewer votes).

The app does not access other users' accounts. Personal data of third parties is not collected, sold, used for advertising or shared.

3. YouTube API Services

GGame Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google processes data is explained in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time on the Google account permissions page.

4. Meta (Instagram and Facebook)

Instagram and Facebook operations use Meta's official Graph API and are subject to the Meta Privacy Policy.

5. Where is data stored?

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on a computer owned by GGame. During upload, videos are placed temporarily in cloud storage and deleted right after publishing. Statistics are kept for content planning and deleted on request.

6. Comments and votes

In choice series, public comments on our own posts are read only to count "A" or "B" votes. Usernames are not stored or published.

7. Your rights and contact

For questions about your data or deletion requests, write to contact@ggame.com.tr. We respond within 30 days at the latest.
