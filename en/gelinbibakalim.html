Who are we?

A small studio that loves curiosity.

Gelin Bi Bakalım is a production of GGame, an independent game and content studio. Our goal is simple: make videos that make you stop scrolling for a minute, say "hmm", and then want to send them to a friend.

Our stories are made with creative tools and an AI-assisted production pipeline; each one is checked for language, pronunciation and accuracy before it goes live.

  • ✦No politics, no polarisation: content for everyone.
  • ✦Every claim in our fact videos is verified with at least two sources.
  • ✦Our stories are fiction; we don't use the names of real people or organisations.
  • ✦We read the comments; many new topics come from you.
