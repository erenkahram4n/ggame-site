About us

Small team, big curiosity.

GGame is an independent game and digital content studio founded in Türkiye. In games and in videos we chase the same thing: catch people in a few seconds, then stay on their minds.

We create our content with creative tools and an AI-assisted production pipeline, but every video is checked for language, pronunciation and accuracy before it goes live.

  • ✦New content every day on Instagram, YouTube and Facebook.
  • ✦Content for everyone, free of politics.
  • ✦Every claim in our fact videos is verified with at least two sources.
  • ✦We listen to our community: in choice stories, you decide the ending.
